Generic 21-Population Feudal Start

Standard land-map shell • zero TC idle time • Scout counts toward population
Target: 20 villagers / 21 population. 0 Gold before the Feudal click.
  1. 0:00: Starting 3 villagers build 2 Houses (2 on one, 1 on the other), then join the sheep. Scout finds and pushes sheep under the TC.
  2. Villagers 1–6 (Pop 7): Sheep under the TC.
  3. Villagers 7–9 (Pop 10): Wood; first Lumber Camp.
  4. Villager 10 (Pop 11): First Boar lure.
  5. Villagers 11–12 (Pop 13): First Boar.
  6. Villager 13 (Pop 14): House near berries → Mill.
  7. Villagers 14–15 (Pop 16): Berries.
  8. Villager 16 (Pop 17): Second Boar lure.
  9. Villagers 17–18 (Pop 19): Second Boar.
  10. Villagers 19–20 (Pop 21): Wood.
  11. Loom: At 20 villagers where the civ/opening requires it; then click Feudal immediately once 500 Food is available.
The civ-specific pages now show exactly where each civilization departs from this shell. Gold is added during the Feudal transition according to the unit plan.

Fast Castle 26–28 Villagers

Fast Castle with enough wood for Market + Blacksmith + first Castle production
Target: Click Feudal around 26–28 villagers with roughly 13–15 Food / 5–6 Wood / 5–6 Gold; during transition move 3–5 villagers to Gold and keep 6–8 on Wood.
  1. Open standard food economy; do not overspend on Feudal military unless required to survive.
  2. Reach 200 Gold while pushing the food count high enough to bank 800 Food.
  3. Add the Market + Blacksmith during the Feudal transition unless the civ uses a different Castle trigger/building.
  4. Move enough villagers onto Gold to hit 800F + 200G efficiently; keep at least 6–8 Wood for Farms, production and the Castle-age transition.
  5. At Castle, immediately place the key production building: Stable, Monastery, Range, Siege Workshop, or civ-specific structure.
26 villagers is the aggressive end; 28 is safer and gives more wood/food for the post-Castle transition.

Full Water Map Start

Dark Age Dock + fishing economy; no Dark Age mill unless the map forces it
Target: 20–21 villagers by the Feudal click, with roughly 6–7 on Food, 6–7 on Wood, 3–4 Fishing Ships, and 0–2 Gold depending on whether you plan Galleys, Hulks or a land transition.
  1. Villagers 1–6: Sheep under the TC. Keep the food compact to avoid walking.
  2. Villagers 7–10: Wood. Get the Lumber Camp down early. The current fishing economy is wood-hungry because Fishing Ships still cost 75 Wood and their shore/deep-fish gather rate was reduced by 15%.
  3. Villager 11: Lure the first Boar back to the TC.
  4. Villager 12: Build House → Dock → second House. Queue the first Fishing Ship immediately.
  5. Villagers 13–14: Wood. Keep at least 5–6 effective woodcutters while the Dock is producing; add a third/fourth woodcutter before floating for more ships.
  6. Villagers 15–20: Food under the TC / Boar / Deer. Do not build the Dark Age berry Mill just because it is in the land build; the Dock is replacing that early food investment.
  7. Fishing Ships: Aim for roughly 3–4 before Feudal if the fish are safe. Loom after the second/third ship and click Feudal around 20–21 villagers as execution allows.
  8. Feudal transition: Move 4–6 food villagers to Wood and 3–4 to Gold. Add a second Dock if contesting water; bank for your first warships rather than continuing unlimited Fishing Ships.
  9. Water choice: Hulks cost 75 Wood + 35 Gold and counter Fire Ships but are vulnerable to Galleys; Galleys are the ranged counter to Hulks. Hulks also benefit from Blacksmith melee attack upgrades. The current 185872 patch reduced Hulk-line armour and HP, so treat them as specialised counters rather than an armoured all-purpose fleet.
Safe default: fish boom first, then contest water. Aggressive version: 19–20-pop Feudal with 2–3 Fishing Ships and a faster second Dock/warship timing.

Hybrid Map Start

Early Dock + 2–3 Fishing Ships, then convert the fish economy into land pressure
Target: 19–21 villagers at Feudal with about 7–8 Wood, 8–10 Food, 0–2 Gold in Dark Age and 2–3 Fishing Ships supporting the land economy.
  1. Villagers 1–6: Sheep. Keep the first food core on the TC so villager production remains continuous.
  2. Villagers 7–10: Wood; build the Lumber Camp.
  3. Villager 11: Build House → Dock → second House on the pond/shore. Start Fishing Ships immediately.
  4. Villagers 12–14: Wood/food. Build toward 6–7 on Wood so the Dock can produce 2–3 Fishing Ships without delaying your land infrastructure.
  5. Villagers 15–18: Food, using Boar/Deer where possible. Keep the Town Center running without relying on the pond as your only food source.
  6. Villagers 19–20: Wood or Gold according to your land plan. A Scout rush needs more Food; Archers need more Wood; Knights need Gold.
  7. Loom + Feudal: A clean aggressive build can click around 19–20 villagers with 2–3 Fishing Ships. A safer 20–21-villager click gives more food/wood for the land army.
  8. During transition: Aim for roughly 11–13 Wood, 3–4 Gold and only a small Food group because the Fishing Ships are now supplying food. Send one villager toward the opponent's pond to scout/contest their Dock.
  9. Feudal: If the opponent has a Dock, make the appropriate counter fleet (for example Galleys/Hulks depending on their composition); otherwise spend the water income on your land production. A Barracks + Range/Stable transition is normally more valuable than trying to win water with one or two uncommitted ships.
Current community build orders also use a 19-pop hybrid framework with about 3 Fishing Ships before Feudal, then a heavy Wood transition. The important principle is to make the Dock pay for itself without allowing the water investment to delay your land army.
